About

Nicholas Falzon —
working photojournalist, Malta.

I’m Nicholas Falzon, a freelance sports photographer and photojournalist based in Malta. I’ve spent my career on the sidelines of the beautiful game, working with professional football clubs both here at home and across Europe.

My main goal is to make sure players and clubs walk away with the high-quality content they actually deserve. Whether I’m shooting a match-day gallery or helping a player build their personal brand, I focus on delivering sharp, impactful images that tell your story the right way.
